# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
- Primary: Cooperative Managers and Administrators, tracking overall inventory, logistics, maintenance orders, finances, and rental statuses.
- Secondary: Logistics, warehouse, and maintenance staff who execute field updates, check-ins, and diagnostics.

## Product Purpose
To manage the complete lifecycle of cooperative equipment (home-care assets) from entry, stock, reservation, installation, retrieval, hygiene, maintenance, to disposal, ensuring that every asset's history is transparent and auditable.

## Positioning
The absolute source of truth for cooperative assets, ensuring no asset is double-allocated, and all transitions are backed by auditable business actions.

## Brand Personality
Operational, reliable, and precise. High-density layouts, clean neutrals with dark-tinted bases, and high-visibility status tags.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, poorly-aligned legacy spreadsheets/tables.
- Over-rounded SaaS-cream cards nested in cards.
- Weak text contrast on colored tags.

## Design Principles
1. **Immutable Traceability**: No asset state changes happen by accident or direct edits. All transitions must be driven by business actions and logged.
2. **Dense Operational Utility**: Optimize layout density and spacing for rapid scanning of tables, timelines, and dashboards.
3. **Action-Oriented Context**: Disable arbitrary direct field edits; guide the user through clear operations like "Open OS", "Initiate Transport", or "Retire Asset".

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target WCAG AA color contrast ratios (min 4.5:1 for body copy).
- Full keyboard support for core table filters and action modals.
- Screen-reader-friendly state badges.
